A Historic Institution Lagos Island, Nigeria

MORE THAN A SCHOOL

A legacy that
stands the test of time.

Founded on 20 September 1909, King's College Lagos is one of Nigeria's historic secondary schools, known for its longstanding educational tradition.

Over the years, the College has been part of the educational journey of generations of students. Its story reflects the importance of learning, character, discipline, and service to society.
